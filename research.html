Research

From how children build a story to tools clinicians can trust.

My research sits where language science meets clinical practice. I adapt and validate narrative language measures, study how developmental language disorder is identified in Mandarin-speaking children, and develop dynamic, evidence-based approaches to assessment and intervention — with a persistent question running underneath: do our measures mean the same thing across Mandarin and English?

Themes

Four connected lines of work

01 · Measurement

Narrative language assessment

Adapting and validating narrative language-sampling measures for Mandarin-speaking children — building reliability and validity evidence to support a clearly specified interpretation and use, rather than assuming a tool transfers across languages unchanged.

02 · Diagnosis

Identifying DLD in Mandarin

Community-based, multi-phase and multi-method work on identifying developmental language disorder in Mandarin-speaking children — with attention to classification accuracy and the real differences between screening, assessment, and diagnosis.

03 · Intervention

Dynamic assessment & intervention

Developing dynamic assessment and evidence-based narrative intervention, and asking what works, for whom, and how change is best measured — attending to dosage, fidelity, and generalization rather than efficacy claims alone.

04 · Equity

Cross-linguistic & bilingual validity

Whether our measures mean the same thing across Mandarin and English, so that bilingual and Chinese-background children are represented fairly — and difference is not mistaken for disorder. A recurring theme, and an implementation concern, across the work.
