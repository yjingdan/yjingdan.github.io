Research Overview

I adapt and validate narrative language measures for Mandarin-speaking children, study how developmental language disorder is identified, and develop dynamic approaches to assessment and intervention. A recurring question across this work is whether measures function equivalently across Mandarin and English.

Research Areas

Narrative language assessment

Adapting and validating narrative language-sampling measures for Mandarin-speaking children.

Identifying DLD in Mandarin

Community-based, multi-phase and multi-method identification of developmental language disorder.

Dynamic assessment and intervention

Dynamic assessment and evidence-based narrative intervention, including how change is measured.

Cross-linguistic validity

Fair assessment for bilingual and Chinese-background children, distinguishing difference from disorder.
