About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Language Science and Technology at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, and a bilingual (English / Mandarin) speech-language therapist. My work combines active research with extensive clinical practice to bridge theory and application, with the aim of improving communication outcomes for Mandarin-speaking children.

My research adapts and validates narrative language measures and develops evidence-based, dynamic approaches to assessment and intervention. Clinically, I have provided individual and group therapy across the lifespan — with a focus on Autism Spectrum Disorder, developmental language disorder, social communication, and AAC — across a special education setting, private clinics, and hospitals.
